Living Room VR

Walk through photoreal, Blender-baked houses and get them guest-ready: fluff the pillows, reshelve the books, straighten the art, vacuum, water the plants and take out the trash. 120 Blender-generated living rooms across 20 houses.

VR (Quest, PC VR)Left stick: walk · Right stick: snap turn · Push right stick forward: teleport · Grip: grab (works at a distance) · Trigger: use / switch · Y/B: task list on your wrist
Keyboard & mouseW A S D walk · mouse look · E/click: grab, use, place · Q/right-click: drop or throw · Tab: tasks · 1–6: jump to a room · H: back to the front door · Shift: hurry · Esc: pause. Inside this preview the mouse may not lock: drag to look, click to use.
Xbox / PlayStation / Switch controllerLeft stick: walk · Right stick: look · A/✕/B(Switch): grab & use · B/○: drop · Y/△: tasks · Menu/Options/+: pause
Phone & tabletLeft joystick: walk · drag the right side: look · USE: grab / use · DROP: drop or throw · TASKS button: checklist
Smart TV remote↑ ↓ walk · ← → turn · OK: use · Back: drop · a paired gamepad works too
